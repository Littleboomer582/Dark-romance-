How to Play

Phone / tablet

  • Left side: drag to walk (a joystick appears under your thumb).
  • Right side: drag to look around.
  • Tap to place the selected block. Press and hold to dig.
  • ▲ jump / swim / fly up, ▼ sneak / fly down. In Creative, tap FLY or double-tap ▲.
  • Tap a hotbar slot to pick a block; ▦ opens your inventory and crafting.

Computer

  • Click to capture the mouse. WASD move, Space jump, Shift sneak, Ctrl sprint.
  • Left mouse dig, right mouse place, wheel or 1–9 pick blocks.
  • E inventory, F fly (Creative), Esc pause, F3 debug info.

Survival

  • Blocks you dig go into your inventory. Placing a block uses one.
  • Craft planks, glass, bricks and lamps in the inventory. Coal fuels the furnace recipes.
  • Falls hurt. Your hearts refill slowly. Lamps light up the night and caves.
